Not shipped, provided by the Jellyfin host at runtime
-----------------------------------------------------
Jellyfin.Controller and Jellyfin.Model (GPL-3.0-only) are referenced with
ExcludeAssets=runtime, so they come from the installed Jellyfin server rather
than from this package. Their transitive dependencies (Microsoft.Extensions.*
and Entity Framework Core, MIT; Polly, BSD-3-Clause; ICU4N and J2N, Apache-2.0;
Diacritics, Apache-2.0) likewise come from the host and are not redistributed
here.

External program
----------------
This plugin invokes ffmpeg as a separate process. ffmpeg is not bundled; it is
resolved from the Jellyfin server's own installation or from PATH, and it
carries its own license (LGPL-2.1-or-later by default, GPL when built with
--enable-gpl).
